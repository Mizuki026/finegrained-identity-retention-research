\section{Scope of the Supplement}

This supplement reports the complete preregistered pair family, measurement
coverage, experimental lineage, and claim boundaries for the main paper.  It
contains no newly selected analysis and no identity-linked qualitative media.
All identifiers are formal pseudonymous pair identifiers.  Frozen source
artifacts, their byte counts, hashes, and claim-to-evidence mappings are listed
in the accompanying traceability package.

from the frozen intervention-analysis table.  For each role, B, T, and S denote
the baseline, target-projection-exchange, and orthogonal-sham present fractions.

\begin{equation}
 E=\tfrac{1}{2}\big[(S_{\mathrm{ctrl,T}}-S_{\mathrm{ctrl,S}})

\section{Blind Annotation and Outcome Construction}

The private annotation packet contained 3,360 anonymous sampled frames.  It
mixed attributes, pair roles, conditions, and seeds and did not expose the
private mapping.  Annotators A and B agreed on 1,521 states; all 1,839
disagreements were adjudicated before unblinding.  Annotator independence is a
researcher attestation and cannot be established from the two submitted CSVs.

The authoritative unblinding record is the corrected v2 record.  The correction
changed persisted schema handling, not labels, conditions, seeds, samples,
frame indices, thresholds, or outcomes.  Outcome construction pools the four
seeds and seven sampled frames for each unit-condition.  U states stay missing;
there is no imputation, no reduced panel, and no regenerated trajectory.

\section{Execution and Verification Facts}

The monolithic transaction acquires 40 reference units once and constructs 640
leave-one-pair-out token directions.  It generates 480 trajectories and 24,000
scheduler steps in 35.559 GPU-hours.  The separately authorized VAE-only private
decode produces 480 videos, each with 49 frames at $720\times480$ and 8 fps, in
0.742 GPU-hours.  No interpolation or upscaling is used.

Independent verifiers check task enumeration, schema, direction hashes,
projection invariants, persisted files, byte counts, SHA256 values, media
contracts, coverage, outcomes, and pair-effect recomputation.  Raw identity
embeddings, LFE tensors, and direction tensors are not persisted by the
scientific runner.  Runtime and analysis packages are frozen read-only after
verification.

\section{Interpretation Ledger}

\paragraph{Verified facts.}
The panel and transaction counts, projection/sham numerical invariants,
annotation counts, coverage, and all 20 pair effects are bound to frozen
CSV/JSON artifacts and independent verification records.

\paragraph{Fixed-panel observations.}
The observational windows show mixed LFE/token signs and descriptive linkage;
the controlled intervention yields mostly ties, four wrong-direction effects,
and two correct facial-hair effects.  These statements describe the fixed
20-pair panel.

\paragraph{Accepted working models.}
``Representation--readout mismatch/reorganization'' and ``multi-path,
distributed representation'' are scientifically useful models compatible with
the results.  They are not directly verified mechanisms.

\paragraph{Unverified mechanism hypotheses.}
Nonlinear downstream use, query/state-dependent routing, partial off-manifold
movement, compensation through the spatial reference route, and measurement
saturation remain possible.  The current experiment does not distinguish them.

\paragraph{Decision.}
The evidence rejects a broad claim that the tested complete-family linear LFE
projection is a robust causal controller under the frozen dose and panel.  It
does not justify information absence, route irrelevance, necessity,
sufficiency, bottleneck localization, population generalization, or
cross-attribute ranking.

\section{Privacy and Release Boundary}

Public or submission artifacts may include manuscript source, aggregate tables,
pseudonymous pair identifiers, protocols, source code, verification records,
and hashes.  They must not include references, identity names, aligned faces,
face embeddings, LFE/direction tensors, blind mappings, annotation frames,
private latents, decoded videos, or any reference-corresponding generated
video.  This supplement intentionally contains no qualitative identity panel.
