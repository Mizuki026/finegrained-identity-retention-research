## Immediate interpretation decisions

1. Should frozen human consensus be accepted as the main finite-panel
   eyeglasses description?
2. Should the audited YuNet plus CLIP path be retained only as secondary
   convergent measurement evidence?

## Mechanism-design questions for the next stage

These are prospective questions, not present findings:

- Which ConsisID code objects implement reference-image preprocessing, global
  identity embedding, local face features, feature projection, and injection
  into the video transformer?
- At which transformer blocks and denoising timesteps are reference-derived
  features applied?
- Can target-attribute information be decoded from the reference-side and
  denoising-side representations before output failure appears?
- Are failed fixed units associated with reference representation loss,
  injection weakness, or later suppression during denoising?
- Which training-free intervention is the smallest credible test: target-region
  reference masking, local-feature reweighting, identity-condition scale,
  injection-layer ablation, or denoising-timestep scheduling?
- What negative controls distinguish attribute-specific effects from generic
  identity or image-quality changes?

## Required design gate before any new GPU run

Before a probe, intervention, ablation, or generation run, freeze:

- the exact hypothesis;
- ConsisID code-to-concept mapping;
- eligible fixed samples and exclusion policy;
- treatment, control, and negative-control definitions;
- layer/timestep intervention targets;
- seed policy and compute budget;
- attribute, identity, video-quality, and observability metrics;
- thresholds and missingness handling;
- analysis unit and multiplicity policy;
- failure handling and stopping rules;
- code commit, environment, and weight hashes.

## Prohibited shortcuts

- Do not infer an internal ConsisID bottleneck from output metrics alone.
- Do not describe detector replacement as improved generation.
- Do not use frames as independent experimental units.
- Do not rank eyeglasses against facial hair from the current heterogeneous
  measurement paths.
- Do not choose an intervention using favorable outcomes and then call it
  prospective.
- Do not modify or overwrite the formal panel, frozen annotations, repaired
  measurement outputs, or their hashes.
- Do not selectively regenerate zero- or negative-difference units.
- Do not publish the reference-linked generated videos.
- Do not draft a manuscript without a new researcher request.

## First safe autonomous action after human decisions

Perform a read-only ConsisID structure audit and produce a proposed mapping from
the abstract information-flow stages to actual modules, functions, tensors, and
call sites. This step should not modify ConsisID, load GPU weights, inspect new
outcomes, or launch generation. Any proposed instrumentation must be reviewed
before implementation.
