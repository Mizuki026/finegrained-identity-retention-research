# Pilot v1.1 Analysis and Claim Plan v1

Status: prospective and frozen before attribute-result inspection.

## Scientific unit and estimands

The analysis unit is one generated video within one predetermined attribute condition. Frames are repeated within-video observations used for temporal description and observability accounting only. The Pilot has four videos per attribute and identity/attribute confounding; it is not a confirmatory sample.

Primary descriptive outputs, separately by attribute and sample, are valid/unobservable/pipeline-failure fractions; frozen metric summaries over valid frames only; expected-label match fractions where defined; temporal variability and adjacent valid-frame flips; raw frame sequences; and reason codes.

No overall cross-attribute retention score, frame-level inferential test, post-hoc threshold, regression coefficient, causal claim, or formal significance test is authorized for this Pilot.

## Claim rules

| Claim ID | Planned wording if evidence is produced | Minimum evidence | Prohibited strengthening |
|---|---|---|---|
| C-P1 | In this fixed Pilot, the frozen instrument produced these observable-frame summaries. | Complete raw frame table, video summary, independent verification | The model generally preserves/loses the attribute. |
| C-P2 | Some Pilot videos showed different descriptive trajectories under the frozen measure. | Raw sequences plus coverage and reason codes | This demonstrates H1. |
| C-P3 | The Pilot is compatible with a need for a larger controlled study. | Descriptive heterogeneity and stated confounds | Any causal or architecture-specific explanation. |
| C-P4 | Reference audits support use of instruments on their frozen references. | Existing reference-audit artifacts | General or generated-video-domain validation. |

## H1-H4 preservation

- H1-H4 remain hypotheses after the Pilot, even if descriptive results are heterogeneous.
- H2 requires prespecified attribute-property measurements and a controlled/matched formal design.
- H3 requires output evidence followed by separately authorized probes and reproducible interventions.
- H4 requires a larger matched design; the Pilot cannot separate hair granularity from identity, domain, lighting, and label confounds.

## Missingness and formal-study trigger

Report coverage first. A low-coverage video is not excluded; valid-only metric summary is labelled low coverage. No interpolation, forward fill, imputation, or conversion of missing to absence is allowed.

Before a paper-level formal study, create a new preregistration freezing target population, sampling frame, identities/seeds, matched attribute design, reference-quality criteria, prompt/seed policy, generation budget, exclusions, endpoints, statistical model, multiplicity, uncertainty reporting, stopping rule, and claim boundary. The current Pilot cannot be promoted by reanalysis alone.

Every manuscript sentence must be mapped to a claim-evidence record. Facts cite artifacts; diagnostics state limits; inferences are qualified; hypotheses remain future tense; and research decisions name their protocol/gate.
