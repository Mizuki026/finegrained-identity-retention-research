# Scalar linkage window closure v1

## Status

The researcher accepted all five scientific interpretations in `token_quantile_scientific_interpretation_proposal_v1.md`. The global scalar outcome-linkage window is therefore closed after the completed primary, representation-secondary, injection-RMS, cosine, and token-RMS quantile families already frozen in their respective evidence packages.

## Verified facts retained

- The token-RMS quantile family contains 108/108 defined secondary Spearman coefficients over ten fixed matched pairs per attribute and passed independent full recomputation.
- Eyeglasses has 54/54 positive coefficients; facial hair has 30 positive and 24 negative coefficients.
- q10, q50, and q90 show similar coarse sign/grid structure; the observed fixed-panel pattern is not descriptively exclusive to q90.
- No p-values, confidence intervals, token identities, derived concentration measures, new frame family, intervention, model execution, or GPU execution were added.

## Accepted scientific interpretation

1. The evidence is more compatible with a broad token-magnitude ranking shift than an exclusively high-magnitude-token phenomenon, without proving uniform participation or excluding sparse attribute-relevant tokens.
2. Magnitude and direction alignment are separable readouts. The eyeglasses RMS/quantile pattern together with mostly negative mean H–R cosine supports representation–readout mismatch, not a causal update rule.
3. Facial-hair linkage is state- and statistic-dependent. Hidden-state normalization is a plausible but unisolated contributor.
4. Multi-path, distributed representation remains the most robust working model, not a causally verified mechanism.
5. Further global scalar-correlation expansion has diminishing mechanism value; the scalar linkage window is closed rather than automatically opening the 13-frame × 36-cell family.

## Still unverified

- Whether the magnitude pattern is distributed across many tokens or carried by a sparse attribute-relevant subset.
- Whether `rms(H)` normalization explains the facial-hair contrast between absolute token quantiles and relative RMS.
- Whether late-block negative facial-hair associations reflect correction, compensation, state dependence, or measurement geometry.
- Whether the spatial reference-latent path compensates for local-route patterns.
- Whether any route, block, denoising stage, token subset, or CFG branch is necessary or sufficient.

## Research decision and next gate

The next mechanism direction is token-resolved direction analysis or a prospectively controlled intervention. This decision sets scientific priority only. It does not authorize implementation, new raw-vector capture, per-frame linkage, derived concentration metrics, model loading, GPU tracing, intervention, ablation, or generation. Any such work requires a separate preregistered design and explicit authorization proportional to its computational and scientific scope.
