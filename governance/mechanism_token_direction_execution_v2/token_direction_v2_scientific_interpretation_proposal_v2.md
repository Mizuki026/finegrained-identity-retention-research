# Token-direction v2 scientific interpretation proposal

Status: **proposal for researcher review; not yet an accepted scientific interpretation**.

## Verified facts

1. The authorized complete fixed-panel transaction finished with 40 formal units, 160 fresh preprocessing calls, and 200 direct LFE calls. It produced all preregistered families: 40 same-input direct-repeat controls, 120 four-label equivalence comparisons, 20 current pooled margins, 20 descriptive historical comparisons, 640 token margins, 64 token-panel summaries, and 20 pair-token summaries. Both in-memory independent recomputation and persisted verification passed.
2. All 40 `D1` versus `D2` same-input controls and all 120 seed-label comparisons were bit-exact; their maximum absolute and RMS differences were zero. Thus the four seed-labelled copies collapsed to identical LFE tensors within this loaded process and fixed execution order.
3. The current pooled family did not numerically reproduce frozen run-003 within the preregistered tolerance: 0/20 comparisons passed. Absolute differences ranged from `9.72207635641098e-06` to `0.00032501283567398787`. All 20 retained the frozen sign. Run-003 remained an immutable descriptive comparator and was not used as a v2 validity gate.
4. Eyeglasses pooled margins were positive/correct for 5/10 pairs and negative/wrong for 5/10. Across all 320 eyeglasses token margins, 145 were positive and 175 negative, with no ties. Across the complete 32-token panel, per-token correct counts ranged from 0/10 to 10/10: 6 token indices had more than five correct pairs, 10 had exactly five, and 16 had fewer than five. Twenty-nine of 32 token indices contained both signs across pairs, while all 10 pairs contained both signs across token indices.
5. Facial-hair pooled margins were positive/correct for 6/10 pairs and negative/wrong for 4/10. Across all 320 facial-hair token margins, 173 were positive and 147 negative, with no ties. Per-token correct counts ranged from 1/10 to 9/10: 16 token indices had more than five correct pairs, 4 had exactly five, and 12 had fewer than five. All 32 token indices contained both signs across pairs, and all 10 pairs contained both signs across token indices.
6. No raw LFE tensor, normalized token vector, learned direction vector, aligned image, keypoint, latent, frame, or video was persisted. No outcome was opened or joined, no association was computed, and no transformer step, diffusion trajectory, generation, or intervention was run.
7. Unstructured InsightFace fallback messages appeared in runtime stdout. No raw stdout log or task identifiers were persisted, so no count or unit-, pair-, role-, or attribute-level assignment is claimed. They remain a run-level limitation and did not trigger exclusion or rerun.

## Current fixed-panel observation

The self-contained v2 transaction is internally measurement-valid under its preregistered equality gates, while its token-resolved held-out directions are heterogeneous across both learned token index and matched pair. The complete families contain mixed signs overall; eyeglasses additionally contains two 10/10-positive token indices and one 0/10-positive index, while facial hair contains no uniform-sign token index. This is an outcome-blind readability observation, not evidence that a token contains an attribute or contributes causally to generation.

## Proposed scientific interpretation decisions

1. **Accept v2 as a valid current-transaction token-direction measurement, while preserving historical numerical non-replication.** The bit-exact controls support using the complete current v2 family as one internally coherent measurement transaction. The 0/20 strict historical matches and 20/20 sign matches must both be retained; v2 neither repairs nor replaces run-003.
2. **Accept token-index-specific and pair-specific directional heterogeneity as the main descriptive result.** The complete panels contain mixed positive and negative held-out margins across token indices and pairs. This supports heterogeneity of the frozen linear directional readout, but does not prove semantic token specialization, spatial localization, uniform participation, sparsity, or attribute content.
3. **Interpret the eyeglasses extremes only as compatibility with index-specific readout structure, not as selected “attribute tokens.”** The full 32-index distribution includes both uniformly positive and uniformly negative indices on the ten fixed pairs, while 29/32 indices remain mixed. These extremes are retained as part of the full family and must not be promoted to a best-token subset, semantic label, causal site, or intervention target without a new prospective rule.
4. **Interpret facial-hair results as broadly state/pair-dependent directional alignment at this readout.** All 32 token indices and all 10 pairs contain both signs, while the pooled family is also mixed. This is compatible with representation/readout mismatch or reorganization, but does not distinguish hidden-state normalization, cross-token interaction, spatial-path compensation, measurement heterogeneity, or any causal mechanism. The descriptive difference from eyeglasses is not a cross-attribute difficulty ranking.
5. **Keep the distributed multi-path model as the accepted working model, not a verified mechanism, and close the observational token-direction stage.** V2 probes only an outcome-blind local-LFE linear readout. It does not test the spatial reference-latent path, route interaction, compensation, necessity, sufficiency, bottlenecks, or generated retention. If these five interpretations are accepted, the next stage should be a separately preregistered controlled intervention with prospective target/control rules fixed before any execution; no intervention is authorized by this proposal.

## Mechanism-model implication

The strongest update is about readout structure rather than information loss: a stable current LFE tensor can yield token- and pair-dependent linear directions, so failure of one pooled or scalar readout cannot establish disappearance of attribute information. At the same time, the present signs alone cannot show that information is distributed, used by the generator, or recoverable by another route. A causal intervention would be needed to move from directional readability to contribution.

## Claim boundary

These results do not establish attribute loss, semantic or spatial token localization, sparse or uniform token participation, a bottleneck, causal mediation, route necessity or sufficiency, multi-path compensation, population or model-wide behavior, frame independence, generated-video retention, or cross-attribute ordering. The 640 margins are structured measurements over 20 fixed matched pairs, not 640 independent samples.
