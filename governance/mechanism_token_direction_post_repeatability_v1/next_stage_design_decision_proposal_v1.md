# Post-repeatability next-stage design decision proposal v1

Status: **draft for researcher decision; not preregistered and grants no implementation or execution authority**.

## Scientific state motivating the decision

- Token-direction scientific run-001 remains a nonreusable failed transaction because its frozen historical pooled-LFE replication gate failed before any 640-margin family was persisted.
- The complete direct-vs-hook diagnostic showed bit-exact equivalence when both paths used the same preprocessing output.
- The complete fresh-preprocessing diagnostic showed bit-exact D1/D2 and P1/P2 LFE outputs within one current process, while both current pooled replicate families still failed strict historical run-003 numerical replication but retained every sign.
- The unresolved remainder is therefore a cross-run measurement-state difference. Further scalar or repeated-preprocessing debugging has declining mechanism value and cannot itself answer whether token-resolved directions exist.

## Recommended route

Close the current historical exact-replication diagnostic window as unresolved but bounded. Do not repair or resume v1 scientific run-001 and do not replace frozen run-003. Instead, prospectively preregister a separately named **token-resolved directional analysis v2** whose scientific quantities and validity controls are constructed self-consistently within one newly authorized current-runtime transaction.

This is a new prospective experiment, not a reinterpretation of failed run-001. Historical run-003 remains an immutable prior result and may be reported only as a separate numerical non-replication limitation, not as the v2 pass/fail gate.

## Seven proposed design decisions

1. **Close historical source localization.** Record the exact run-003 numerical mismatch as unresolved cross-run measurement state; do not spend another GPU diagnostic solely trying to identify the old state unless new independent evidence makes that scientifically necessary.
2. **Create a separately versioned v2 experiment.** Do not call it a retry, repair, continuation, or recovered run-002 of token-direction v1. Keep v1 run-001 permanently failed and nonreusable.
3. **Preserve the complete frozen panel and crossing.** Retain all 40 units, ten positive/control pairs per attribute, and all 160 seed-labelled source records. Do not select units, pairs, token indices, attributes, or favorable preprocessing results.
4. **Use a self-contained current-runtime measurement state.** Acquire all required LFE tensors transiently in one loaded process and construct both current pooled controls and token-index directions from that same transaction. Require shape, finiteness, four-label equality, and fixed direct-repeat controls. Compare with run-003 descriptively, but do not require numerical equality and do not replace run-003.
5. **Retain the full 640-margin token family.** Compute exactly `2 attributes × 32 token indices × 10 held-out pairs`, plus the already specified 64 token summaries and 20 pair summaries. No best-token selection, token weighting, threshold tuning, p-values, confidence intervals, or cross-attribute ranking.
6. **Keep the experiment outcome-blind and privacy bounded.** Do not access, join, or correlate retention outcomes. Do not persist raw LFE tensors, identity embeddings, reference copies, decoded media, or per-person feature vectors; persist only pseudonymous margins and bounded summaries.
7. **Keep intervention sequential.** Controlled intervention may be drafted only after v2 is independently verified, frozen, scientifically interpreted, and accepted. Token indices observed in the same v2 data cannot automatically become confirmatory intervention targets without a prospective validation or complete-family rule.

## Why this route is preferred

The current diagnostics establish strong within-process repeatability but not historical byte-level numerical reproducibility. Requiring run-003 equality again would likely continue measuring cross-run state rather than token organization. A self-contained prospective v2 preserves complete-family discipline and allows the originally planned representation question to be tested without erasing the failed history or silently relaxing a threshold.

## Alternatives not recommended as the default

- **Another cross-process repeatability diagnostic:** it could further classify runtime variability, but even a pass would not recover the historical state or answer the token-direction question.
- **Treat sign stability as if v1 passed:** scientifically invalid because the frozen numerical gate failed.
- **Replace run-003 with current margins inside v1:** a post-result baseline change and therefore prohibited.
- **Move directly to intervention:** premature because no independently verified token-direction scientific family currently exists.
